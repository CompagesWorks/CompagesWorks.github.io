About us

We don't know the exact date Compages Works started, only that it was around 2025. The team was first called FightPlanner Team, after FightPlanner, the old name of MOSAIC.

On October 3, 2026 it was renamed MOSAIC Foundation. Later, with Tessera about to come out, it became Compages Works. The name comes from the word compage, a whole made of many parts joined together.
